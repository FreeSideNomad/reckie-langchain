## Introduction
This document outlines the vision for a commercial client receivable solution designed to enhance the efficiency of receivables for customers and attract new clients to the bank.

## Purpose
The purpose of this document is to provide clarity on the solution's goals, stakeholders, features, potential risks, and success criteria.

## Business Goals
- Accelerate receivable processing for customers.
- Reduce administrative tasks for customer users.
- Attract new customers by offering a comprehensive receivable solution.

## Stakeholders
- Customer Users: Individuals or entities using the receivable solution.
- Payor Users: Parties involved in the payment process.
- Bank: The institution providing the receivable solution.

## Features and Functionalities
- Automated Invoice Processing: Automatically generate and send invoices to customers.
- Real-Time Payment Tracking: Monitor payment statuses in real-time.
- Integration with Accounting Software: Seamlessly integrate with popular accounting tools.
- User-Friendly Dashboard: Provide a comprehensive view of receivables and payments.
- Customizable Reporting: Generate detailed reports tailored to business needs.
- Payment Reminders: Automatically remind payors of upcoming or overdue payments.
- Multi-Currency Support: Handle transactions in various currencies for global clients.

## Potential Risks and Challenges
- Low adoption rate of the solution.
- Complexity in using the solution.

## Success Criteria
- Increased sales through new customer acquisition.
